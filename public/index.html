Pickup operations

Website access stays available 24/7. Orders are accepted only when the feature flag is enabled, order acceptance is on, pickup is available, and a requested time falls within the published Toronto kitchen hours. Pickup requests remain subject to owner review.

Online ordering also requires FOOD_ORDERING_ENABLED=true in the server environment. Delivery is disabled. Marketplace platforms are not connected.

Payment methods and tax

Customers always remain pending until you approve the order. Enter one manual payment method per line as Name | instructions. Enter one tax jurisdiction per line as CA-ON | 0.13. Orders are blocked for regions without an explicitly configured tax rate. Shipping is arranged after review and is not included in the checkout total.
